Candles poured slowly, to burn slowly

Small-batch soy wax candles made by two people in a workshop in Fort Kochi.

See the candles

How we make it

  1. 01

    Pour

    Soy wax is heated to 85 degrees and poured by hand into each jar.

  2. 02

    Cure

    Every candle rests for two weeks so the scent binds to the wax.

  3. 03

    Trim

    Cotton wicks are trimmed to six millimetres for a clean, steady flame.

Our story

Meera and Joseph started pouring candles in their kitchen in 2019. Today the workshop makes about four hundred candles a month, still one jar at a time.

The candles

Vetiver, Monsoon Earth and Cardamom Smoke, Rs 900 each.
